ShortForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

ShortForge ("the app") turns landscape videos into vertical 9:16 clips on your Android phone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Your videos stay on your phone

  • Videos you open are read and processed only on your device. The app never uploads them anywhere.
  • Your edits (frames, cuts, steering) are saved as small project files in the app's private storage on your phone. Long-press a project on the Home screen, or uninstall the app, to delete them.
  • Exported videos are saved to your phone's gallery (Movies/ShortForge). You decide if and where to share them.
  • The app has no accounts and runs no servers of its own, so it collects no personal information itself.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Your IP address, which can indicate an approximate location
  • Information about how ads are viewed and tapped, and diagnostic data from the ads SDK

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the Pro screen ("Privacy choices"). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Read how Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

"ShortForge Pro" is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. The app only learns whether you own Pro; it never sees your payment details. Pro turns off all ads, so the ads SDK is not used after you buy it.

Permissions

  • Internet: only for ads, consent and Google Play purchases.
  • Storage (Android 8 and 9 only): to save exported videos to your gallery.
  • Videos are opened through Android's system picker, so the app can only see the videos you choose.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
