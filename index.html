How it works

Choose your stream, go play something else.

Pick your OBS recording or paste the link of your own finished YouTube stream. HighlightForge does the rest on your PC.

  1. 1

    Reads the game screen

    Every kill, multikill and teamfight in League; rounds, fights and augments in TFT, pinned to the second.

  2. 2

    Listens to you

    Your best reactions make the cut, and strong swear words are muted word by word.

  3. 3

    Picks the real plays

    Google's Gemini AI (free, with your own key) watches every candidate and keeps the good ones.

  4. 4

    Edits and packages

    Opening hook, game cards, kill pop-ups, 3 thumbnails, 5 titles and a description with chapters.

FAQ

Questions

Which games does it work with?

League of Legends (including ARAM and Arena) and Teamfight Tactics. It reads the game screen of your recording, so it doesn't matter what language you talk in.

Do I need a strong PC?

An NVIDIA RTX graphics card is recommended and makes it much faster. Without one it still works, just slower. You need about 6 GB of free disk.

Does it give me an advantage in the game?

No. HighlightForge never runs during a game and never touches the game client. It only edits recordings of games that are already over. More about fair play.

What is the Gemini key, and does it cost money?

Google's Gemini AI picks the best plays and writes the titles. Its free tier is enough, and you get a free key in a minute. Without one, HighlightForge still works but writes simpler titles.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC".

Click More info → Run anyway. It means Windows doesn't know the installer yet.
